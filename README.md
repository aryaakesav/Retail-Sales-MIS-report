# Retail Sales & Regional Profitability MIS Dashboard
An interactive corporate Management Information System (MIS) dashboard engineered in MS Excel to transform raw multi-region retail transaction datasets into structured corporate business insights.

## Core Financial Metrics
* **Grand Total Sales:** ₹21,250.00
* **Grand Total Gross Profit:** ₹6,038.00
* **Highest Profit Region:** South Chennai (₹1,861.00)
* **Dairy & Eggs Top Segment:** North Chennai (₹1,064.00)

## Technical Features Utilized
* **Data Synthesis:** Dynamic Pivot Tables and multi-variable cross-tab matrix tables to isolate key regional cost drivers.
* **Visual Intelligence:** Pivot Charts mapping product sales distribution and settlement channels.
* **Interactive Controls:** Floating geographic Slicers (`Central`, `North`, `OMR-Velachery`, `South`) for real-time manager auditing.
* **Data Cleanup:** Implementation of proper Indian Rupee (₹) financial formatting and removal of layout gridlines.

## Consumer Insights Delivered
* **Digital Settlement Shift:** Identified a dominant 58% consumer preference shift toward digital checkout methods, led by Credit Cards (30%) and UPI (28%) over Cash on Delivery.
